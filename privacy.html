Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 22, 2026

Sunil Sharma ("we", "us", or "our") operates SwiftFX - Currency Converter (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is and is not collected when you use the App.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not require account creation, login, or registration. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information. We do not build user profiles or track you across other apps or websites.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your device to provide its functionality:

  • Your selected source and target currencies
  • Recently used currencies (up to 5)
  • Appearance preference (light, dark, or system theme)
  • Cached exchange rate data for offline use
  • App interaction counts (used to determine when to show review prompts)

This data is stored entirely on your device using standard iOS storage mechanisms (UserDefaults and local SQLite database). It is never transmitted to us or any third party by the App.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect data independently:

a) RevenueCat (Subscription Management)

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions. RevenueCat may collect an anonymous app user identifier, purchase and subscription history, and device identifiers. RevenueCat does not receive your name, email, or personal details from the App.

RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

b) Google AdMob (Advertising)

For users on the free tier, the App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (including IDFA if you have granted permission), IP address, device model and operating system information, and ad interaction data. AdMob uses this information to serve ads, including personalized ads where permitted.

Premium subscribers do not see advertisements, and the AdMob SDK does not serve ads for them.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your device's settings: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Google AdMob Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

c) Frankfurter API (Exchange Rates)

The App fetches currency exchange rates from the Frankfurter API, a publicly available service providing European Central Bank reference rates. These requests transmit your device's IP address as part of standard internet communication. No user identifiers or personal information is sent.

Frankfurter: https://frankfurter.dev

d) Analytics

The App may send anonymous usage events (such as screen views and feature usage counts) to an analytics service. These events do not contain personally identifiable information. They are used solely to understand how features are used and to improve the App.

4. App Tracking Transparency

On iOS 14.5 and later, the App requests your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before allowing AdMob to access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). If you deny this permission, ads will be served in a non-personalized manner. You can change this setting at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children. If you believe a child has provided data through one of our third-party services, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

6. Data Retention

All locally stored data persists on your device until you delete the App. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data. Data collected by third-party services (RevenueCat, Google AdMob) is retained according to their respective privacy policies linked above.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including under GDPR and CCPA), you may have the following rights:

  • Right to know: This policy discloses what data is and is not collected.
  • Right to deletion: Uninstall the App to remove all locally stored data. For data held by third-party services, contact them directly using the links above.
  • Right to opt out of personalized advertising: Use your iOS tracking settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).
  • Right to contact us: Email us at for any privacy-related requests.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
